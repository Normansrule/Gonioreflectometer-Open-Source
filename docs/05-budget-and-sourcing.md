## "Own stock" lines (count $0 if you have them)

18 AWG silicone wire, perfboard, JST-XH pre-crimped kit, metric screw assortment, M3 heat-set
inserts, a resistor or two, a 12 mm pushbutton — $39.14 together. Buy only what's missing.

## Ways to restore the HQ camera within $500

The HQ upgrade costs $41.75 on top of the $478.15 stock path ($519.90). Any two of these close the gap:
a used HQ camera, the SKR from BTT's AliExpress store (often cheaper than the BIQU store price used
here; not confirmed), skipping the Edmund mirror (already on the budget path), or campus loans
(ColorChecker, wattmeter, oscilloscope).

## Rules

- Every phase is tracked against its cap; if a phase would go over, a cheaper substitute is proposed first.
- Prices are labelled approximate with the check date, in the CSV and in docs.
- Prefer printing over buying wherever stiffness allows (all brackets, pulleys, hubs, housings).
- Optional lines (gray card, ColorChecker, wattmeter, Pi 5 cooler) are never counted — borrow them.
