# Power budget and Requirement 1

**Requirement 1:** one certified wall plug, high efficiency, no exposed mains, sized for the final
machine with ≥ 25 % headroom.

| Requirement | How it is met | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| One plug-in, externally certified adapter, no exposed mains | MEAN WELL GST120A24-P1M: UL62368-1, DOE Level VI, IEC C14 inlet | datasheet; mains never enters our enclosures |
| Final-machine budget with ≥ 25 % headroom | 74.4 W peak allocation vs 120 W → 61 % headroom | `tools/power_budget.py --check` (runs in CI) |
| 5.1 V from a synchronous buck ≥ 90 % efficient, ≥ 5 A, real datasheet | Pololu D42V55F5, 5.0 V ±3 %, 6 A. **Efficiency not yet confirmed at 24 V** (Pololu: 80–95 %) | acceptance test T5 |
| Input fuse, reverse polarity, master switch, latching e-stop on motor power only | 6.3 A T fuse, IRF4905 P-FET, Bulgin rocker, IDEC e-stop on the motion board feed | `hardware/power/README.md` §4, T7–T9 |
| Wall-plug efficiency and idle draw estimated | 85 % typical; 5.8 W asleep (below) | T10 |
| Sleep mode disables drivers and LED when idle | Klipper `idle_timeout` → `OG_SLEEP` after 5 min | T10 |

**Two honest deviations from the brief:** the rail is 5.0 V, not 5.1 V (no 5.1 V module exists in this
class — the short 18 AWG lead keeps the Pi above 4.9 V, checked in T4), and the ≥ 90 % buck
efficiency is a measured pass/fail item rather than a datasheet number.

**Deeper idle:** shutting the Pi down (Pi 5 with `POWER_OFF_ON_HALT=1` in the EEPROM) leaves only the
SKR logic and adapter overhead, about 1–2 W at the wall; the master rocker gives 0 W.

Generated by `tools/power_budget.py` from `data/power_loads.yaml`. All six phases installed, Raspberry Pi 5 assumed (worst case).

**Headroom at peak:** adapter rated 120 W vs peak 74.4 W -> 61 % headroom (required >= 25 %): **PASS**.
Peak current 3.10 A of 5.00 A available.

**Buck check:** 5 V allocation peak 5.85 A against a 5.5 A rating. The allocation assumes the Pi drawing its full 5 A USB budget at the same moment the servo stalls; with no USB peripherals the realistic peak is about 13 W + 3.75 W = 16.8 W (3.4 A).

Buck and adapter efficiencies are design estimates (see the YAML) and are replaced with bench numbers in the Power module acceptance test.
