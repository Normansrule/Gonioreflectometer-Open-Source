# References

## Theory
- P. Hanrahan, *Reflection Models I: Ideal Materials*, CS348b Lecture 10, Stanford, Spring 2016 (BRDF definition, reflection equation, reciprocity, isotropy, energy conservation, Fresnel/Schlick, Lambert).
- B. Walter, S. Marschner, H. Li, K. Torrance, *Microfacet Models for Refraction through Rough Surfaces*, EGSR 2007 (GGX).
- C. Schlick, *An Inexpensive BRDF Model for Physically-based Rendering*, Computer Graphics Forum 1994.
- P. Debevec, J. Malik, *Recovering High Dynamic Range Radiance Maps from Photographs*, SIGGRAPH 1997.
