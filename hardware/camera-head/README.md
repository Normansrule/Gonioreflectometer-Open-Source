# Camera Head module (Phase 1, M1.6)

Status: **planned** — full documentation is written when this module's milestone starts (one module at a time, with your approval before moving on).

## 1. Purpose
Holds the HQ Camera (or Camera Module 3) square to the arm and aimed at the centre; black PETG shell and lens baffle.

## Interface spec
2 x M3 on 20 mm to the arm end. CSI ribbon (500 mm) routed along the arm and through the pivot axis. Optical axis passes within 0.5 mm of the measurement centre (shimmed during calibration).

## What later phases attach
Phase 5: servo-driven polarizer wheel clips onto the lens barrel.
