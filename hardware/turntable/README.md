Status: **planned** — full documentation is written when this module's milestone starts (one module at a time, with your approval before moving on).

## 1. Purpose
A4: platter on an 8 mm spindle in two 608s inside the hollow centre post, 20T to printed 80T (4:1), AS5600 on the spindle end, printed height-adjustable stage.

## Interface spec
Replaces the Phase 1 turntable-height spacer on the centre post OG-40. Stage top = measurement centre height. Harness M-A4, E-A4, I-A4.

## What later phases attach
