# Light Ring module (Phase 3, M3.1)

Status: **planned** — full documentation is written when this module's milestone starts (one module at a time, with your approval before moving on).

## 1. Purpose
A3: 12 in lazy Susan carrying the light tower, GT2 belt teeth-out around a printed rim (~24:1), printed idlers on 608s, AS5600 on the motor shaft, home flag at 0 deg.

## Interface spec
Sub-plate bolts to the 4-hole ring footprint. Top plate carries the light bracket OG-40 at r = 140. Harness M-A3, E-A3, I-A3 through a spiral-wrapped service loop (no slip ring).

## What later phases attach
Collision model limits mid-elevation poses to about +/-120 deg (light arm meets the camera arm beyond that).
