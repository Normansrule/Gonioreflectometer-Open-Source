# Polarizer Fill Kit module (Phase 5, M5.1)

Status: **planned** — full documentation is written when this module's milestone starts (one module at a time, with your approval before moving on).

## 1. Purpose
Polarizer film on the lamp, servo-rotated polarizer on the camera (parallel/crossed for specular separation), diffuse fill light (second CRI-95 COB behind a diffuser), printed ChArUco scanning mat.

## Interface spec
Film slot in the lamp housing; servo clip on the camera head; SERVO to SKR PA1; L-FILL to SKR FAN2.

## What later phases attach
Phase 6 uses cross-polarized captures to separate diffuse and specular reflectance.
