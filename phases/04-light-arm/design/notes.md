# Design

- 12 in lazy Susan on a plywood sub-plate; arm bracket on the top plate (OG-40, r = 140 mm).
- GT2 belt teeth-out on a printed rim → ~24:1 (set `gear_ratio` from the measured rim).
- Home flag at 20°; roller over-travel switch just beyond, wired into J3 [R1].
- Light 300 mm from the zero point.

Fasteners: #8 × 5/8 wood screws × 8, M4 × 12 × 4, M5 × 10 + T-nuts × 4, M8 × 40 × 2 (idlers), M3 × 8 × 4.
